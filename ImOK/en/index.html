Use it with clear expectations

A time-based check-in, not an emergency service

The app is for sharing a check-in. It does not replace emergency services and does not guarantee delivery.

  • The phone must be switched on.
  • You need a network connection to send and update the check-in.
  • Notifications must be allowed for an alert to appear.
  • If any of these conditions is missing, the update may not arrive or appear.

Straight answers

Frequently asked questions

How does it work?
Choose your contacts, confirm that you are well from the app or widget, and they can check the status you share.
Who receives the update?
It is shared with the contacts you choose and who accept the invitation.
Can I send a message or photo?
Yes. You can add a message, a photo, or use statuses such as “I'm here” and “I have arrived”.
What if the phone is off or has no network?
The app needs the phone to be on, connected to a network and allowed to show notifications. Otherwise, the update may not arrive or appear.
Can I send an SMS or WhatsApp message?
Yes. You can open the message and choose SMS or WhatsApp manually. The app does not send it by itself.
Is it an emergency service?
No. It is a time-based check-in, does not guarantee delivery and does not replace emergency services.
