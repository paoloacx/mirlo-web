Questions Answered

Frequently Asked Questions

Direct answers to common questions.

How does it work?

You choose your contacts, confirm that you are well from the app or widget, and they can view the status you share.

Who receives the update?

It is shared exclusively with the contacts you pick and who accept your invitation.

Can I send a message or photo?

Yes. You can add a custom message, a photo, or quick statuses like “I'm here” and “I have arrived”.

What happens if the phone is off or has no network?

The app needs the phone to be powered on, have cellular/Wi-Fi coverage, and allow notifications. Updates will send as soon as coverage is restored.

Can I send updates via SMS or WhatsApp?

Yes. You can open the update and share it directly through SMS or WhatsApp with family members who don't have the app.

Is it an emergency service?

No. It is a personal daily reassurance tool. It does not guarantee delivery and does not replace emergency dispatch (911/112).
