What it does

A clock to organize your day

Every routine is a slice of the clock. Your whole day, always in sight.

Your free gaps

How it works

Three steps and you're set

Getting started takes a minute.

1

Create your routines

Pick a name, icon, color and time. They can repeat or happen just once.

2

See them on the clock

Each routine gets its own slot. Tap one to complete, skip or edit it.

3

Follow your progress

Check your streaks, your hours and your full day in the timeline.

Straight answers

Frequently asked questions

Is it free?
It's free to download and Premium expands the features.
What happens to my data?
Your routines stay on your device and in your iCloud. There are no accounts and no ads. Full details are on the privacy page.
Do I need Health or the calendar?
No. They're optional and only turn on if you allow them.
Does it work on iPad?
Yes, it works on iPhone and iPad.
Which languages does it speak?
English, Spanish, German, French and Italian.
How do I get help?
Write to us from the support page.
