Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear, straightforward answers.

Is Poetry Companion free?

The app includes a free option to write, analyze meter, and organize poetry books. An optional Pro upgrade is available for creators who want expanded features.

Where are my poems stored?

Your poems are stored on your device and in your iCloud, and you can use the app completely locally without sharing your poems.

Can I write poems entirely by hand without suggestions?

Absolutely. The app serves as a full literary notebook: enjoy a clean editor, distraction-free focus mode, live syllable counts, and rhyme lookups to compose at your own pace.

Does it work offline?

Yes. You can write, read your books, edit verses, and browse past drafts at any time without internet. Only external dictionary queries or optional suggestions require connectivity.

Can I hear how my poem sounds?

Yes. You can record your own voice reciting each poem to check musicality, pauses, and cadence alongside the written text.
