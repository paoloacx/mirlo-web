Frequently asked questions

Answers regarding features, data privacy, and availability.

What is My Health Keeper?

It is a private personal health companion for iOS designed to organize clinical records, analyze reports, manage medication schedules, and correlate your wellbeing with environmental and physiological metrics.

Which AI models can I choose from?

You can switch between Apple Intelligence (100% on-device on compatible iOS 26+ devices), Google Gemini 3.8 Flash, and Groq. You stay in control of whether computations run offline or via secure APIs.

What makes the report extraction «faithful»?

The analysis prompt is strictly instructed never to extrapolate or hallucinate unwritten clinical data. It separates confirmed from suspected diagnoses and reflects only the care plan explicitly stated by the attending physician.

Where is my data stored?

All information is stored locally on your device via SwiftData. Mirlo Apps operates no storage servers and has zero access to your data.

Does this app replace medical advice?

No. My Health Keeper is an objective record-keeping and educational tool. It does not diagnose medical conditions or change prescriptions. Always consult a licensed medical provider for clinical decisions.

When will it be available for download?

The app is currently in App Store review with Apple. As soon as review completes, it will be published on the App Store for iPhone and iPad.
