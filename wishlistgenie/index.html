Good to know

Frequently asked questions

What is Wishlist Genie?

Wishlist Genie helps you save gift ideas, organize them into lists, and share those lists with friends or family.

How can I add a product to a wishlist?

Add a product from a link, search for it on the web, or import details from a screenshot. You can also add an idea manually.

Can I share a wishlist with other people?

Yes. Invite people to a shared list so you can add and update wishes together. The list activity view shows changes made by its members.

Can I organize wishes with my own tags?

Yes. Create custom tags, assign them to wishes, and manage your tags in the app.

Does Wishlist Genie work on iPad?

Yes. Wishlist Genie supports iPad and includes layouts adapted for iPad and iPhone Duo.
